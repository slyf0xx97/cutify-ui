# Fonts

Type carries most of a cute UI's voice. Pick one display face with character and one plain, readable body face, plus a mono for data if the page has numbers or IDs. Vary the pairing between products; don't reuse Pixelify Sans or Anybody everywhere.

Every font below is on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (checked on 31 Oct 2026: each family's folder is under `ofl/` in the google/fonts repository and its `OFL.txt` is present). Loading from Google Fonts needs no attribution. If you bundle the files, keep `OFL.txt` with them. For privacy, self-host (see `references/themes.md`, "Fonts and privacy").

## Readability rules

- Never set paragraphs in a display, pixel or handwritten face. Body copy is 16px or more in a body face.
- Pixel faces look crisp only at multiples of their design size: Silkscreen at 8, 16 or 24px, Jersey 10 at about 20, 30 or 40px. Press Start 2P is too wide for more than two or three words.
- Single-weight display faces (Mochiy Pop One, Bagel Fat One, Dela Gothic One, Madimi One) belong on headings of 20 to 28px or more.
- Gaegu runs small and thin: use 22px or more at weight 700. Patrick Hand is the more readable handwriting swap at 16px.
- Bricolage Grotesque's quirky details disappear under about 16px.
- Outfit's light weights are too thin on coloured fills; use 400 or more.
- Playpen Sans is wide; cap line length with `max-width: 62ch`.
- Label caps in mono need letter-spacing (`.08em` to `.14em`) and a size of at least 10px.

## Not OFL or unverified

- Chewy and Kosugi Maru are under the Apache License 2.0 in google/fonts: free to use, but not OFL. Fine to use; note the different licence.
- M PLUS Rounded 1c was not found under `ofl/` when checked. Confirm its licence on its specimen page before using it.
- Luckiest Guy was not checked.
- Never use a font from a free-fonts download site unless its licence file says what you can do with it.
