    Why does the Calvin cycle need ATP?

    Hoot AI

    Think of ATP as a charged battery.

    1. The light reactions charge the batteries.
    2. The Calvin cycle spends that charge to glue CO₂ into sugar.
    3. No charge, no glue, so no sugar.

  3. 3

    Get quizzed

    Pip writes questions from your topics. A wrong answer gets a one-line reason, then a second try later. Topics you miss go to Shelly. Why: pulling an answer out of memory makes it stick better than rereading.

    Come back at the right time

    Shelly looks at what you missed in Pip's quizzes. Shaky topics come back sooner, solid ones later. You set which days you're free, and you can move any session. Why: short reviews spread over days beat one long cram.

How it works

Your notes travel from tutor to tutor. Each one adds a single job and hands it on. Nothing gets scheduled without your free days. Why: three small jobs are easier to trust than one tutor doing everything.
