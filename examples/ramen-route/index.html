1

Station 1: Take the ticket

CustomerYou
Customer pays at the machine→You write firmness and seat→Ticket goes on the rail

What you do

  1. Greet the customer and take the ticket from their hand.
  2. Ask "Noodles firm, normal or soft?" and write the answer on the ticket.
  3. Write the seat number, then clip the ticket to the end of the rail.

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeYou ask about firmness but don't write it down, so the noodle station guesses.

✓ FixWrite it on the ticket before the ticket leaves your hand.

Sample tally: 3 remade bowls

When it's busy

A line forms at the door. Take tickets while people wait, so the kitchen starts sooner. Seat people in ticket order, not in the order they reach the counter.

2

Station 2: Cook the noodles

You
Next ticket off the rail→One basket, one timer→Drain hard

What you do

  1. Take the oldest ticket from the rail and clip it to a basket handle.
  2. Loosen one portion (140 g, sample) into the basket and start that basket's timer.
  3. When it beeps, lift and shake hard five times so no water goes in the bowl.

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeOne timer for several baskets, so the ones that went in later come out soft.

✓ FixOne timer per basket, with the ticket on the handle.

Sample tally: 9 remade bowls

When it's busy

Up to six baskets go in together. The water cools, so the same noodles take about 30 seconds longer. Wait for the boil to come back before the next basket goes in.

3

Station 3: Pour the broth

You
Warm the bowl→Tare, then broth→Noodles in, fold once

What you do

  1. Fill the bowl with hot water to warm it, then tip it out.
  2. Read the ticket, add one ladle of the right tare (30 ml, sample), then the broth.
  3. Slide the drained noodles in and fold them once so they don't clump.

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeShoyu tare goes into a shio bowl when bowls are lined up.

✓ FixEach bowl sits on its own ticket from the moment it comes off the shelf.

Sample tally: 4 remade bowls

When it's busy

Put tare in the bowls while the noodles boil, so the broth is ready when the timer goes. Keep the bowls in ticket order, left to right.

4

Station 4: Add the toppings

You
Read the ticket again→Same order every time→Extras last, on top

What you do

  1. Read the ticket out loud: "Shoyu, extra egg."
  2. Go clockwise from the top: chashu, egg, scallion, naruto.
  3. Put any extra on last, on top, so it's the first thing the pass sees.

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeThe customer paid for an extra egg and it never goes on.

✓ FixRead the ticket out loud before the first topping, and add extras last.

Sample tally: 11 remade bowls

When it's busy

Several bowls arrive at once and you start working from memory. Don't. Look at each ticket, even when the last five were the same.

5

Station 5: Check at the pass

You
Bowl against ticket→Wipe the rim→Ring and call the seat

What you do

  1. Check tare, firmness and extras against the ticket.
  2. Wipe the rim with a clean cloth.
  3. Ring the bell once and call "Seat 5, shoyu, extra egg."

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeA bowl waits under the lamp and the noodles go soft.

✓ FixIf a bowl has waited 30 seconds (sample), whoever is nearest carries it.

Sample tally: 6 remade bowls

When it's busy

This is where bowls back up. Two or three can wait at once. Send the oldest first, and say the seat number every time, even if it feels obvious.

6

Station 6: Serve the counter seat

YouCustomer
Two hands, over the counter→Say the order back→Take the ticket

What you do

  1. Carry the bowl with two hands to the seat on the ticket.
  2. Set it down and say "Shoyu with extra egg."
  3. Take the ticket off the counter and drop it in the done box.

Where it goes wrong

✕ MistakeThe right bowl goes to the wrong seat.

✓ FixServe by the seat number on the ticket, never by who looks like they've waited longest.

Sample tally: 5 remade bowls

When it's busy

Seats fill and empty out of order. Check the seat number twice: once at the pass and once at the counter.
