      How it works

      1. 01Customers order You, customer

        Each order lands in the bakery's list with an address and what's in the box.

      2. 02The planner suggests an order Code

        Nearest stop next, measured along the streets. It shows how much riding that saves.

      3. 03The baker checks and sends You, baker

        Drag or nudge stops, then send the rider. The order locks once the bike leaves.

      4. 04You follow the bike You, customer

        The map, the four steps and the ETA move with the rider until the box is at your door.
