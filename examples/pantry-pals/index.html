    How it works

    AI suggests → AI lists → You approve

    1. 1Fill your fridge YouYou: Tap what you have at home.
    2. 2Plan the week AIChef: Seven dinners that use your fridge first.
    3. 3Build the list AIShopper: Everything the plan needs that you don’t have.
    4. 4Approve YouYou: Check it all in one place, then say yes.
