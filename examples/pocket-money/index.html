Pocket Money

A savings app for kids aged 8 to 12. The weekly allowance lands, the jar fills toward one goal, and a parent says yes before any money gets spent.

  • Save for one real thing
  • Watch the jar fill every Friday
  • A parent approves each spend

    How it works

    Follow one Friday's coin

    1. 1ParentDad sets the allowance

      $10 a week in this sample. He can change it any time.

    2. 2AppFriday, it lands

      The app adds it at 08:00 and shows Mia the split.

    3. 3KidMia drops her share in

      Her goal sets the jar share. The rest goes to her spend pocket.

    4. 4AppThe jar fills

      One step up the glass, and one Friday fewer to the bike.

    5. 5ParentSpending waits for a yes

      Mia asks, Dad approves or says not this time.
