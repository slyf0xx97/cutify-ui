        How it works

        1. 01You pick topics You

          Comics, poetry, food and five more. One is enough.

        2. 02The Curator plans AI

          It picks the tables that fit best and orders them so the walk stays short.

        3. 03You star and approve You

          Add tables you spot on the map. The route re-plans around them.

        4. 04You print the map You

          One sheet with the floor, the walk and every stop.
