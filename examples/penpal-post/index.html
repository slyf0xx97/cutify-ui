Sample letter
JP

Dear friend,

Thank you for your first letter. My English is still a little, so please be patient. Yesterday it rained, so I stayed home with my cat, Mochi (もち). She sleeps on my desk when I write.

In autumn I like to eat sweet potato. 焼きいも is so good. Please tell me about your town.

From Aiko

    How it works

    1. 1

      Read Aiko’s letter You

      She writes in English and adds a little Japanese.

    2. 2

      Write your reply You

      Your words, with one reply idea from the Postie if you want it.

    3. 3

      See it side by side AI

      The Translator puts Japanese next to each line, with gentle notes.

    4. 4

      Pick stickers AI You

      The Postie marks two that fit; you choose.

    5. 5

      Send when ready You

      Confirm, and the letter folds up and flies to Aiko.
