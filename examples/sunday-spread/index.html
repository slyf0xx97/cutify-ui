    How it works

    1. 1You invite friends You

      Set the day and tick who's coming. That's the head count.

    2. 2Guests claim dishes Guests

      Each guest picks something to bring, in their own words.

    3. 3The Host checks the spread AI

      Flags double-ups and empty courses, then suggests a dish to someone free.

    4. 4The Baker sizes recipes AI

      Scales each claimed recipe to the head count and adds a tip.

    5. 5You confirm the menu You

      Review every dish and who's bringing it, then confirm.

    Who's who

    H Host AI

    Pink headscarf, clipboard. Keeps the table balanced and suggests dishes. Never claims for anyone.

    B Baker AI

    Chef's hat, green apron. Scales each recipe to the head count.

    Y You You
