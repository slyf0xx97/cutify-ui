      How it works

      1. 1You snap blocks into a program

        Forward, turn left, turn right and repeat. Each level has a block limit.

        You
      2. 2Bolt follows them, one block at a time

        The block Bolt is doing lights up, so you can spot where a plan goes wrong.

      3. 3Missed twice? Bolt gives a hint

        A nudge about the idea, like counting squares or spotting a pattern. Never the answer.

        AI
      4. 4Reach the battery, add a brick

        The level is solved and your tower grows by one brick.

        You

      BBolt AI

      A small robot helper. Follows your blocks, and after two missed tries gives a hint that points you the right way without solving it.

      YYou You

      The coder. You choose every block, press Run, and decide what to change when Bolt misses.

      In this sample, Bolt’s hints are written ahead of time.
